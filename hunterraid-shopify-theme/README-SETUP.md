# HunterRaid Shopify theme – setup

1. Shopify admin → Online Store → Themes → Add theme → Upload zip → choose HunterRaid-shopify-theme.zip.
2. Products → Add product "HunterRaid Projector Hunting Game" with ONE option ("Bundle") and 3 variants, in this order:
   - 1 Set — Starter Pack: price 109.99, compare-at 150
   - 2 Sets — Family Pack: price 202.99, compare-at 310
   - 3 Sets — Ultimate Pack: price 294.99, compare-at 470
   Product images are optional; without them the theme shows the built-in HunterRaid photos.
3. Add two more products (no shipping needed is fine, untick "This is a physical product"):
   - Shipping Protection – 7.99
   - 1-Year Warranty – 7.99
4. Online Store → Themes → Customize → HunterRaid product page section:
   pick the main product (needed for the home page), the Shipping Protection product and the Warranty product. Save, then Publish.
5. Settings → Policies: fill in Refund, Privacy and Shipping policies (the footer links to them).
